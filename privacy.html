Privacy Policy – Pterodactyl Clip Uploader

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Controller

Pterodactyl, Leon.treu67@gmail.com

2. What data the tool processes

  • TikTok authorization data: the access token and refresh token issued by TikTok after the operator signs in, and basic account information (TikTok open ID, display name) returned by the user.info.basic scope.
  • Video files: the operator's own edited clips, which are sent to the operator's TikTok inbox.

The tool does not collect data about viewers or any other TikTok users.

3. Purpose

The data is used only to upload the operator's videos to the operator's own TikTok account as drafts via the TikTok Content Posting API.

4. Storage

Tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer only. They are not sent to any third party other than TikTok, and they are not stored on this website. This website itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the operator revokes the app's access in the TikTok app (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions) or deletes the local token file.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact: leon.treu67@gmail.com
