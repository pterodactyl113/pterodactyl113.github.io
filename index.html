Pterodactyl Clip Uploader

Pterodactyl Clip Uploader is a private tool used by a single creator to send their own edited short-form gaming clips (vertical videos with subtitles) from their computer to their own TikTok account.

How it works:

  1. The creator signs in with TikTok and authorizes the app.
  2. The tool uploads a finished video file to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft (Content Posting API, upload mode).
  3. The creator opens the TikTok notification, adds the caption and disclosure settings in the TikTok app, and publishes the post themselves.

The tool does not post automatically, does not access other users' accounts, and does not collect data from viewers.
